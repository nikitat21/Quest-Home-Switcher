# v2.2 · A fresh look. Your own atmosphere.

[← Overview](../README.md) · [Download](DOWNLOAD.md) · [Installation](INSTALLATION.md)

The same Home Switcher, with a more personal feel.

- **Three glass finishes:** Clear, Frosted and Solid, refined across Official Homes, Custom Homes, buttons and dialogs.
- **A little atmosphere:** animated Snow, Space and Lava lamp backgrounds — or None. Motion has its own switch.
- **Appearance, one tap away:** try the new controls at the top of the app. Clear + Snow is the default; existing saved choices stay yours.
- **Smoother details:** animated Official/Custom transitions, cleaner rounded hover feedback and a new Cascadia preview showing its current skybox.
- **Safer recovery handling:** improves recognition of an already-restored original Home after an interrupted switch, without discarding recovery backups or skipping integrity checks.

**Already using QHS?** Use the in-app update or install over your existing app. Your pairing, settings and downloaded Homes stay in place. Both Home Libraries are unchanged; no Home re-download is needed for this update.

**First install from a PC:** `adb install -g Quest-Home-Switcher-2.2.0.apk`

[Explore Appearance →](APPEARANCE.md) · [Installation guide →](INSTALLATION.md)
