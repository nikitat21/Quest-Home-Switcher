# Your own atmosphere

[← Overview](../README.md) · [What’s new](RELEASE_v2.3.md) · [Help](SUPPORT.md)

Open **Appearance** at the top of the app. Changes are visible immediately and saved for your next visit.

| Setting | Choose your look |
| --- | --- |
| **Glass finish** | **Clear** for transparent surfaces and soft highlights, **Frosted** for blurred glass, or **Solid** for a calmer opaque panel. |
| **Background** | **Snow**, **Space**, **Lava lamp**, or **None**. These effects belong to the Switcher panel; they do not change your Quest Home. |
| **Motion** | Turn animation on or off independently of the chosen look. |
| **Seasonal effects** | On by default. Automatically show the current holiday, or switch off to keep your usual background all year. |

**Clear + Snow** is the new default for fresh installations and updates without an existing Appearance selection. Saved choices are kept, whether you update in the app or from a PC.

The background continues moving while you adjust Appearance. With **None**, the glass finish still applies. Prefer the simplest look? Choose **Solid + None**.

## A little seasonal atmosphere

**Seasonal effects** follows the Quest's local calendar. No holiday-specific download or app update is needed:

| Season | When it appears |
| --- | --- |
| **Halloween** | October 25 through November 1. |
| **Christmas** | December 1 through January 6. |
| **Easter** | Palm Sunday through Easter Monday; the dates are calculated each year. |

During a season, its scene temporarily replaces the usual background, even when that background is **None**. Effects do not mix. Your chosen glass finish and motion setting still apply; after the season, your normal background returns.

**You stay in control.** Switch Seasonal effects off to keep your normal background. Off stays off across restarts and updates. Turn it back on whenever you like: the current season appears immediately, or your normal background stays visible until the next season.

Choosing a background yourself during a season takes priority for the rest of that year's occurrence. Seasonal effects stays enabled for the next season. To restore the current season sooner, switch Seasonal effects off and back on.

The app checks the date when opened or resumed and periodically while its panel is active. It does not keep a background service running just to watch the calendar.
