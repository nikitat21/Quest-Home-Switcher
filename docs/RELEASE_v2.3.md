# v2.3 · A little seasonal atmosphere.

[← Overview](../README.md) · [Download](DOWNLOAD.md) · [Installation](INSTALLATION.md)

Halloween, Christmas and Easter now arrive automatically in the Switcher panel — without needing a separate app update every holiday.

- **Automatic by default:** one simple **Seasonal effects** switch in Appearance. Turn it off or back on whenever you like.
- **Your choices are kept:** your usual background returns after the season; glass and motion settings stay yours.
- **Manual choices take priority:** choosing another background skips the rest of that season's occurrence, not every future holiday.
- **A new Easter scene:** animated eggs, butterflies and tulips join Halloween and Christmas.

**Already using QHS?** Update in the app or install over your existing app. Pairing, settings and downloaded Homes stay in place. Both Home Libraries are unchanged; no Home re-download is needed.

**First PC installation:** `adb install -g Quest-Home-Switcher-2.3.0.apk`

[Season dates and controls →](APPEARANCE.md) · [Installation guide →](INSTALLATION.md)

Existing firmware compatibility limits remain unchanged. This appearance update does not add new Quest firmware support. [Compatibility and limits](COMPATIBILITY.md).
